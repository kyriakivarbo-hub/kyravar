Meet Our Expert Team

At Medicalbuddies, we understand the challenges and triumphs of moving abroad to build a medical career. As international medical graduates (IMGs) ourselves, we've experienced every step of the journey — from NHS job applications and interviews to adapting to a new healthcare system while preparing for postgraduate exams.

Our mission is simple: to empower IMGs through one-to-one mentorship that is personal, practical, and honest. We are not a large agency — we're doctors helping doctors, offering the kind of real-world support we wish we had when we started.
